随机森林–LightGBM 动态多因子选股与 HMM 择时的量化投资策略 Random Forest–LightGBM Dynamic Multi-Factor Stock Selection with HMM Market Timing

项目基于沪深 300 成分股构建量化策略：HMM 识别五类市场状态并生成择时信号，随机森林从 32 个候选因子中筛选重要性前 10 的因子，LightGBM 回归预测合理市值并筛选低估股票，每 10 日调仓。运用 Silhouette、BIC 与回测结果评估状态划分、收益表现与风险控制。 A quantitative strategy on CSI 300 constituents: an HMM identifies five market regimes and generates timing signals; Random Forest screens the top 10 of 32 candidate factors; LightGBM predicts fair market value to select undervalued stocks, with rebalancing every 10 days. Silhouette scores, BIC, and backtesting were used to assess regime quality, returns, and risk control.
