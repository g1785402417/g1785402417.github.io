FEATURED PROJECT · 01

随机森林–LightGBM 动态多因子选股
与 HMM 择时的量化投资策略Random Forest–LightGBM Dynamic Stock Selection
with HMM Market Timing

Q

项目基于沪深 300 成分股，将 HMM 的市场状态识别、随机森林的因子筛选与 LightGBM 的市值预测组合起来：HMM 划分五类市场状态并生成择时信号，随机森林从 32 个候选因子中筛选前 10，LightGBM 预测合理市值并辅助筛选低估股票，每 10 日调仓。Using CSI 300 constituents, the project combines HMM regime detection, Random Forest factor selection, and LightGBM fair-value prediction. HMM identifies five market regimes and generates timing signals; Random Forest screens the top 10 of 32 factors; LightGBM estimates fair value to support undervalued-stock selection, with rebalancing every 10 days.
